About this piece

Put your steady hands and literary love to the test with this bookish twist on a classic tumbling block stacking game! Shifty Stacks is an original design created by Side Quest Co. It features 3D Printed book blocks! Carefully remove a block and place it on top, just don’t let the stack tumble! Great for family game nights, book club gatherings, or as a charming gift for readers, this game combines skill, strategy, and a love for literature.

The box doubles as an alignment tool for the book blocks. The game cover appearance may vary based on the colours of book spines that are available - but we can promise, it will be adorable no matter what!

Features: 54 3D Printed Book Blocks Ages: 6+ Size: Blocks: 3×1×0.6"; Box Size: 3.14×10.23×3.31"

A classic block stacking game with a literary lover twist! To begin, stack your bookish blocks 3x3 into a sturdy tower, alternating directions with each layer. Then slide out the blocks one by one and place them on top of the stack.

The perfect gift for the bookworms, librarians, and literary enthusiasts in your life!
