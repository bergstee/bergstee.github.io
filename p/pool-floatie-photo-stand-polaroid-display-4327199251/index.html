About this piece

Splash some fun onto your desk or shelf with this super cute 3D printed photo stand! Shaped like a classic pool floatie, it's the perfect way to display your favorite Polaroid or small photo with a playful, summery twist.

Tired of boring photo frames? Our "Pool Floatie" photo stand is an original design by Side Quest Co., born from a love for unique decor and the joy of summer days. We wanted to capture that nostalgic, carefree feeling and turn it into a functional piece of art for your memories. This isn't just any photo stand – it's a little piece of poolside paradise!

Unique Pool Floatie Design: An exclusive, original creation by Side Quest Co. you won't find anywhere else!

Dimensions: Silly Goose - w: 2.3 in x l: 2.2in x h: 2.0in Flamingo - w: 2.0in x l: 1.8in x h: 2.6in Nessie - w: 2.6in x l: 2.5in x h: 2.3in

Perfect for Polaroids: Specifically designed with a slot to securely hold standard sized instant photos (like those from Fujifilm Instax or vintage Polaroid cameras). Also great for other small prints or even business cards!

High-Quality 3D Print: Made from durable, lightweight eco friendly PLA plastic.

Proudly Canadian: Designed, printed, and finished with care right here in Canada by Side Quest Co.

Adds Fun Decor: Instantly brightens up any workspace, bookshelf, nightstand, or gallery wall.

Great Unique Gift: Perfect for photo lovers, pool enthusiasts, teens, college students, your favourite sad millennial, or anyone who appreciates quirky, handcrafted decor.

Each Pool Floatie Photo Stand is brought to life using FDM 3D printing technology. We carefully print each piece layer by layer. As with all 3D printed items, minor layer lines are a natural part of the process and add to the unique character of your stand! We then perform any necessary post-processing to ensure quality.

Care Instructions: To clean, gently wipe with a damp cloth. Avoid exposing to extreme heat (like leaving in a hot car or direct sun on a very hot day) as this can warp the plastic.
