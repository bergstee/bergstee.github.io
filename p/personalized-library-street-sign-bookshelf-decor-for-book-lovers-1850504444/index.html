About this piece

Bring the charm of your favorite books to life with these beautifully crafted Library Street Signs! Perfect for book lovers, bibliophiles, and anyone who adores the magic of literature, these Side Quest Co. original signs are a unique way to celebrate the tropes that you love. Whether you're creating a cozy reading nook, decorating a home library, or adding a whimsical touch to your office, these signs are sure to spark joy and conversation.

Signs are 1.3 inches wide and vary in length from 3-6 inches depending on the number of letters on the sign. Signs come attached to base with silver jump rings and chain.
