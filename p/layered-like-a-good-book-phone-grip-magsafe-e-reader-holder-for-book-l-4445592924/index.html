About this piece

Because the best books, and the best people, have layers. 🎀📚

This sweet heart cake-style phone grip was made for the reader who dog-ears pages, cries at epilogues, and always has one more chapter before bed. Pink frosting, delicate white piped details, and a message that just gets it.

Designed and printed in-house by Side Quest Co., this retractable grip is charming, functional, and frankly too cute to leave at home.
