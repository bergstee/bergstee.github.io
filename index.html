Our process

Most of what we make starts as our own sketch, inspired by a story we love; a few pieces are made under licence from independent designers whose work we admire. Either way it's printed one layer at a time on our own machines, then trimmed, assembled and checked by hand before it ships. Small batches, no warehouse. Just a print studio and a very full bookshelf.
